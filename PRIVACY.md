# InstaPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

InstaPlayer is a Chrome extension that adds playback controls (seek, speed,
mute, fullscreen, Picture-in-Picture) to videos on instagram.com.

## Data we collect
InstaPlayer does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or
sensitive user data.

## How the extension works
The extension runs only on https://www.instagram.com/*. It reads playback
properties of the video on the page (such as current time, duration, volume
and playback speed) locally in your browser, only to display and operate its
controls. This information is not saved and never leaves your device.

InstaPlayer makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking, and
does not read your account details, messages, comments or other page content.

## Permissions
The extension requests no special browser permissions. Its only host access is
the content script that runs on instagram.com.

## Remote code
InstaPlayer does not load or run remote code. All code is included in the
extension package.

## Third parties
InstaPlayer is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed
by Instagram or Meta. Instagram is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
date.
